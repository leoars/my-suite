This installer brings you My Suite {SUITE}: three apps for macOS, simple and straightforward, with the menus and shortcuts you already know.

My Explorer {EXPLORER} · File manager with a folder tree, tabs and a command bar.
My Notepad {NOTEPAD} · Plain-text editor.
My Paint {PAINT} · Image editor.

All three are installed by default. To pick only some of them, click Customize in the Installation Type step.

My Explorer requires macOS {MIN} or later; the other two, macOS 12 or later.
